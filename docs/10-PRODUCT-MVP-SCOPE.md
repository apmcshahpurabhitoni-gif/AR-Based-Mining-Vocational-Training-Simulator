# KAVACH — Product & MVP Scope

**Status:** Pre-freeze specification

## Product objective

KAVACH is a phone-first mining vocational training simulator that teaches and assesses safety procedures through interactive scenarios rather than attendance-only completion.

## MVP must include

- FIRE training module.
- GAS training module.
- Six-step scenario structure per module.
- Interactive 3D training environment for development and current training.
- Replaceable L2 AR presentation path.
- L3 guided fallback.
- Shared data-driven module contract.
- First-attempt behavioural assessment.
- Wrong-action and recovery handling.
- Step-level telemetry.
- G1–G8 qualification gates.
- Cold-retention re-check.
- Offline-capable attempt storage and synchronization.
- QR-backed certificate flow after qualification.
- Hindi content.
- Santali support is required by the target statement but is **not prepared yet**; it remains pending and must be completed before claiming full language compliance.
- Admin/compliance view.

## Explicit non-goals for MVP

- Photorealistic 3D.
- Full physics simulation.
- Multiplayer.
- VR headset support.
- AI-generated safety decisions.
- Computer vision deciding whether a real-world extinguisher was used correctly.
- Large content-authoring CMS.
- Extra modules before FIRE and GAS are complete.
- Unverified machine-translated safety instructions.
- Feature work without training, safety, SIH, or acceptance justification.

## Scope stability

After freeze, a change is admitted only for an SIH requirement, safety correction, existing-contract defect, or acceptance blocker. Visual polish and optional features do not reopen the architecture.
